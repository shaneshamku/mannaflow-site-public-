**5\. Path-Specific Sequences  (for Roman)**  
*Path A — Missed Call, No Contact*

| Day | Channel | Message intent | Sample copy |
| ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- |
| 0 (immediate) | SMS | Acknowledge \+ offer instant path back in | *"Hi, this is \[Company\] — sorry we missed your call\! If you need help with your heating/cooling today, just reply here or tap to book a time: \[link\]"* |
| 0 (evening, if no reply) | SMS | Light second touch, lower pressure | *"Still around if you need us — even after hours. Reply YES and we'll get you booked in."* |
| 1 | SMS \+ Email | Direct ask, remove friction | *"Just checking — did you get your \[furnace/AC\] issue sorted? If not, here's a 2-minute way to book a tech: \[link\]. No obligation to call back and forth."* |
| 3 | Email | Trust content — why respond fast matters | Short, real: what happens if a minor issue goes unaddressed — safety/cost framing, not scare tactics. Ends with booking link. |
| 7 | SMS | Proof \+ direct ask | *"We've helped \[X\] homes in \[city\] this month — average tech arrival same day. Want us to grab you a slot this week?"* |
| 14 | Email | Seasonal hook | Tie to current season (pre-summer AC check, pre-winter furnace tune-up) — "before the first cold snap" framing. |
| 21 | SMS | Close-out, low pressure | *"We'll leave it here for now — if anything comes up with your system, we're one text away: \[link\]. Take care\!"* |

*Path B — Engaged, Didn't Book*

| Day | Channel | Message intent | Sample copy |
| ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- |
| 0 (immediate, agent handoff) | SMS | Reference the specific issue, offer booking directly | *"Hi \[Name\], following up on the \[issue, e.g. 'AC not cooling'\] you mentioned — want me to lock in a tech visit? Here's what's open: \[link\]"* |
| 0 (evening) | SMS | Second nudge only if urgency\_level \= high | *"Just making sure this didn't slip — if your AC is still out, we can likely get someone out today."* |
| 1 | SMS \+ Email | Direct \+ remove objection (cost/timing) | Address the \#1 likely objection captured by urgency\_level/issue\_category (e.g., cost estimate range, same-day availability). |
| 3 | Email | Education tailored to issue\_category | E.g., if issue\_category \= no\_heat: short explainer on common causes \+ when it's same-day vs. schedulable. |
| 7 | Voice callback (human or outbound Retell) \+ SMS | Highest-touch — real callback for warm, qualified leads gone quiet | Short human-toned script: "wanted to check in personally since it sounded urgent." |
| 14 | Email | Proof \+ seasonal | Review/testimonial relevant to their issue category. |
| 21 | SMS | Close-out | Same low-pressure close as Path A, but references their specific issue by name. |

*Path C — Quoted, Went Cold*

| Day | Channel | Message intent | Sample copy |
| ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- |
